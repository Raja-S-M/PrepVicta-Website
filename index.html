Workflow

One loop. Every day.
Until exam day.

PrepVicta compresses 12 months of scattered, inconsistent prep into a tight, efficient, daily system. Here's exactly how it works — no ambiguity.

STEP 01
Set your target score

Enter your exam date and target rank. PrepVicta maps the exact gap between where you are and where you need to be — by subject, by chapter, by sub-topic.

STEP 02
Get your Win Path

Every morning: what to study, in what order, for how many marks. Ranked by impact — not by what's next in the textbook. One task at a time. No decision fatigue.

STEP 03
Study with structure

Sessions are chunked, timed, and followed by recall checks. No passive reading marathons. Active retrieval after every chunk means it actually sticks.

STEP 04
Test, don't just revise

Topic tests lock in learning and update your planner in real time. Every result is a data point — not just a score, but a signal that reshapes your next session.

STEP 05
Track what actually matters

Score trend by week. Accuracy per topic. Planner adherence. Revision cycles completed. None of the vanity metrics. All of the signals that predict your final score.

STEP 06
Repeat until rank is locked

The loop tightens week by week. Weak topics become strengths. Strengths become guarantees. By exam day, nothing on the paper should surprise you.
